Numbering Ranges

VJT 100–499

Private Aviation: private and executive flights. The region is determined by the departure airport.

VJT 500–899

Cargo Operations: cargo transportation. The region is determined by the departure airport.

VJT 900–999

Charter Operations: global range assigned consecutively, without regional subdivisions.

Identification Rules

  • Operational prefix: VJT.
  • OUT and RET are recorded separately; they are not added to the number.
  • The number must be unique across active routes.
  • Private Aviation and Cargo use the departure airport’s region.
  • Use ICAO codes and validate aircraft and airport capabilities.
